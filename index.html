Djembe APK Uploader

A personal build-delivery tool maintained by acrojimmy.

What it does

The uploader transfers a signed Djembe Android APK from a local GitHub Actions runner to the owner's Google Drive. This lets the owner download and test the app on an Android phone.

Each successful upload replaces the latest APK in a private Drive folder. The local build archive retains historical builds for 30 days, with cleanup after successful saves.

Google Drive access

The owner signs in to Google and authorizes the uploader. It requests the limited drive.file permission to create and manage its own upload files. The uploader does not request access to Gmail or contacts.

Uploaded files are private unless the owner changes their sharing settings. APKs and Google authorization credentials are not published on this website.

Privacy and contact

Read the Djembe APK Uploader privacy policy for details about data use, retention, and revoking access.

For general questions, open an issue in the information repository. Issues are public; do not include passwords, tokens, or personal information.

This website provides information about the uploader. It does not offer Google sign-in or host APK downloads.
